BTC Carry Fund live since 9 Oct 2026

The KPK BTC Carry Fund earns a carry on BTC through collateralised lending on blue-chip DeFi protocols. Wrapped BTC is supplied as collateral on Aave V4, USDC is borrowed against it at an automatically managed loan-to-value, and the proceeds are deployed into KPK-curated USDC vaults on Morpho. The fund borrows where the net rate is best: Aave V4 so far, with Spark and Aave V3 being added. Holders stay fully exposed to BTC and earn the spread between stablecoin lending yields and borrow costs, with returns accruing in BTC.

Deposits are in WBTC today; cbBTC is next, and cirBTC follows once Aave lists it. The fund is live and currently funded by KPK, with no performance fee charged yet.

Loop sleeve · optional, not active
An optional syrupUSDG loop at up to 8x on the Aave V4 Maple spoke, taking a capped share of the borrowed stablecoins (25% to 50%) if it is switched on. Leverage capped at 8x. When borrow costs rise above the loop's yield, the loop is partly unwound. The loop sleeve is never relied on to deleverage the BTC leg. It would only start after depositors have run plain BTC deposits and redemptions. It appears here as live once the fund holds a position on that spoke; until then all borrowed USDC sits in the liquid sleeve.

How we manage risk

Automated, watched around the clock, bounded onchain

KPK runs the fund with deterministic agents. They act only within tightly defined onchain permissions and the band and limits KPK sets: when a predefined condition is met, they execute a predefined action. KPK sets the band, tighter in volatile markets, and can trigger an exit itself, as it did on 13 Oct. The agents cannot introduce new strategies, expand exposure beyond approved limits, or bypass risk constraints.

How a signal is handled
Layer 1 · automated
Onchain within one or two blocks

Signals that leave no room for doubt trigger an automated action with no human approval needed, typically executed in under 30 seconds and scoped to the affected market or position, so everything else keeps earning. Exits are graded: a soft shutdown drains a market and can be undone after review; a hard shutdown also sets its caps to zero.

On record: when Cap paused its cUSD stablecoin on 9 June 2026, the exit agent on KPK USDC Yield started draining the affected markets about 15 seconds after the alert ↗. Cap confirmed a false positive minutes later, and the markets were re-enabled after review.
Layer 2 · human-reviewed
The risk team, paged when it matters

Signals where acting automatically would cause false-positive disruption go to KPK's risk curation team, with a page for critical ones. A reviewer decides whether to force a rebalance, shut a market or do nothing. Monitoring is planned with each risk review, so the risks found on paper are the risks watched in production.

The agents
Health-factor managerkeeps HF between 1.15 and 1.25

Holds the BTC leg inside its band, normally 1.15 to 1.25 with a 1.21 target. Monitoring reads the health factor every block and wakes it the moment HF leaves the band; it also runs every 5 minutes as a backstop. KPK sets the band itself: tighter when markets are volatile, looser when they are calm. Below the repay trigger it withdraws USDC from the liquid sleeve and repays; above the borrow trigger it borrows and deploys, split across the two vaults.

Exit agent (emergency unwind)unwinds the whole loan

Withdraws from the vaults and repays all the debt in one transaction. It fires on its own if HF falls below 1.13, or if borrowing costs more than the vaults earn for two 6-hour windows in a row, and switches re-lever off so nothing borrows again until the team re-opens the position.

Agents on the KPK USDC vaultsrebalance, exit, monitoring

Each vault runs three agents through its own permissions layer. The rebalance agent reallocates across approved Morpho markets within tier and cap limits as APYs, flows and utilisation change. The exit agent de-risks a single market on a risk alert such as oracle staleness, liquidity stress, a collateral pause or price divergence. The monitoring agent detects forceDeallocate griefing and restores allocations.

Onchain permissions
No key can move the assets

The Safe holding the position is owned by a contract with no key. Every action passes through a Zodiac Roles permissions layer.

Whitelisted actions only

A fixed set of calls on approved contracts, such as borrowing on the Aave V4 spoke or depositing into the KPK USDC vaults. Anything else is rejected onchain. Each agent and the Manager Safe (2 of 5) get a narrower role.

3-day timelock on changes

KPK's Security Council proposes any change to the permissions, and it waits 3 days onchain before it can take effect. The timelock has owned them since 28 Sep 2026; before that, the Manager Safe set them up during testing.

How the fund scales

Sized by fund size, not by date

Risks and terms

Main risks, and how they are handled

BTC price
Holders keep full BTC exposure; nothing is hedged.
This is the product: carry on top of the BTC held, not a hedge.
Liquidation
A BTC drop faster than the position can be deleveraged.
Monitoring reads the health factor every block. In the normal band the agent repays from 1.15, where BTC would still have to fall 13% to reach liquidation, and the exit agent unwinds the whole loan below 1.13. KPK tightens the band when markets turn volatile.
Negative carry
Borrowing costs rise above what the vaults earn.
The exit agent unwinds if that lasts two 6-hour windows in a row.
Vault liquidity
Thin liquidity in the Morpho vaults can slow deleveraging and redemptions.
At least 25% of the BTC-leg debt stays in the liquid sleeve, and the vault agents rebalance as liquidity changes.
Oracles and pegs
Manipulation, staleness or failure of a price feed; WBTC or a stablecoin off its peg.
Every price is checked against independent monitor feeds, and settlement pauses on a stale or irregular price.
Smart contracts
Aave V4 (Aave V3 and Spark once used), Morpho, the stablecoins, the BTC wrappers (WBTC; cbBTC and cirBTC once live) and the fund's own contracts.
Each one passes KPK's Risk Framework review (more than 150 checks across 12 risk dimensions) and is monitored 24/7. An exploit or pause in a market the vaults lend into triggers an automated exit; other alerts go to the risk team.
WBTC custody
WBTC is backed by BTC held by a custodian.
Its price is watched like every other in the NAV; cbBTC and cirBTC are coming as further deposit assets.
Credit (optional loop)
Only if the optional loop sleeve is switched on: a Maple borrower default can mark syrup tokens down.
The loop is capped at 8x and kept to a share of the stablecoins; it is never relied on to deleverage the BTC leg.
Redemptions
Minutes to hours
for redemptions up to what the liquid sleeve covers, paid at the next settlement cycle
Up to ~3 days
above that: the full settlement window, in normal conditions
Paid in the asset requested. No redemption fee. KPK curates the two Morpho vaults the borrowed USDC sits in and keeps their instant exit liquidity at the level redemptions need. A redemption waits longer only while a price feed is stale or the NAV check disagrees (settlement pauses until it clears), or while paying it would take the loan below a 1.14 health factor, until debt is repaid first.
