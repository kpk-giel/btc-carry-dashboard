How we manage risk

Automated, watched around the clock, bounded onchain

The fund's agents act on rules set in advance, within seconds, and only through onchain permissions. This is the loop they run, and what it has done on this fund so far.

1
Watch
Onchain monitoring around the clock: the health factor and the BTC oracle price, borrowing cost against vault yield, and every venue the money sits in, for paused or frozen contracts, utilisation spikes and exploit signals.
→
2
Decide
Rules set in advance. Inside the band, 1.15 to 1.25, do nothing. Outside it, bring HF back to 1.21. Past the hard limits, unwind.
→
3
Act
The agent sends the transaction itself: repay from the liquid sleeve, re-lever, or unwind the whole loan, usually confirmed within seconds.
→
4
Bounded
Every call passes the permissions layer: whitelisted actions only, and any change to them waits 3 days in a timelock.
Health-factor manager

Keeps HF inside the band, 1.15 to 1.25 with a 1.21 target. It checks every 5 minutes, and the monitoring wakes it within seconds when HF leaves the band. Below the repay trigger it pulls USDC from the liquid sleeve and repays; above the borrow trigger it borrows and deploys.

Exit agent (emergency unwind)

Unwinds the whole loan in one transaction: withdraws from the vaults and repays all the debt. It fires on its own if HF falls below 1.13, or if borrowing costs more than the vaults earn for two 6-hour windows in a row, and switches re-lever off so nothing borrows again until the team re-opens the position.

KPK vault agents on Morpho

The two KPK USDC vaults run their own agents: a rebalancer that moves liquidity between Morpho markets as rates and depth change, and a market-shutdown agent that pulls a vault out of a market and pauses it when that market shows trouble.

Onchain permissions
  • No key can move the fund's assets directly. The Safe that holds the position has a single owner, a contract that holds no key. Every action reaches it through a Zodiac Roles permissions layer.
  • Only whitelisted actions. That layer allows a fixed set of calls on approved contracts, such as borrowing, repaying and supplying on the named Aave V4 spoke and depositing into the two KPK USDC vaults. Anything outside that set is rejected onchain. Each agent gets a narrower role inside that set, and so does the Manager Safe (2 of 5 signers).
  • Changes are slow and visible. The outer set of permissions is owned by a timelock: KPK's Security Council proposes a change, and it waits 3 days onchain before it can take effect.

Risk and liquidity terms

Terms and main risks

Health factor
  • The band: repay below 1.15 back to 1.21; re-lever above 1.25 back to 1.21. The health-factor manager checks it every 5 minutes and acts on its own. From the 1.15 repay trigger, BTC would have to fall 13.0% to reach liquidation.
  • Minimum 1.12: a floor, not a target. From 1.12, a 10.7% BTC drop reaches liquidation.
  • Liquid floor: at least 25% of the BTC-leg debt stays in the liquid sleeve, so the position can be deleveraged quickly after a large BTC drop. Today all of it is there.
Liquidity and fees
  • Redemptions: instant up to what the liquid sleeve covers, paid at the next settlement cycle, normally within minutes to hours. Above that, a settlement window of up to ~3 days. Paid in the asset requested.
  • Fees: a 10% performance fee on gains above a high watermark, measured in WBTC and charged when subscriptions or redemptions settle. No management fee and no redemption fee. KPK's own capital runs fee-free.
Main risks
  • BTC price: depositors keep full BTC exposure.
  • Liquidation: a fast BTC drop, larger than the automations can react to, can lead to liquidation.
  • Smart contracts: Aave V4, Morpho, Maple, the stablecoins (USDC, USDG), the BTC wrappers (WBTC, cbBTC, later cirBTC) and the fund's own contracts.
  • Credit (future, once the loop sleeve is live): Maple lends to institutional borrowers; a borrower default can mark syrup tokens down.
  • Oracles and pegs: WBTC or USDG/USDC moving off their pegs.
