BTC Carry Fund live since 9 Oct 2026

The KPK BTC Carry Fund earns a carry on BTC through collateralised lending on blue-chip DeFi protocols. Wrapped BTC is supplied as collateral on Aave V4, USDC is borrowed against it at an automatically managed loan-to-value, and the proceeds are deployed into KPK-curated USDC vaults on Morpho. Holders stay fully exposed to BTC and earn the spread between stablecoin lending yields and borrow costs, with returns accruing in BTC.

Deposits are in WBTC today; cbBTC and cirBTC deposits will be enabled next. The fund is live and currently funded by KPK, with no performance fee charged yet.

How we manage risk

Automated, watched around the clock, bounded onchain

KPK runs the fund with deterministic agents. They act only within tightly defined onchain permissions and do not exercise discretion: when a predefined condition is met, they execute a predefined action. They cannot introduce new strategies, expand exposure beyond approved limits, or bypass risk constraints.

How a signal is handled
Layer 1 · automated
Onchain within one or two blocks

Signals that leave no room for doubt trigger an automated action with no human approval needed, typically executed in under 30 seconds and scoped to the affected market or position, so everything else keeps earning. Exits are graded: a soft shutdown drains a market and can be undone after review; a hard shutdown also sets its caps to zero.

On record: when Cap paused its cUSD stablecoin on 9 June 2026, the exit agent on KPK USDC Yield started draining the affected markets about 15 seconds after the alert ↗. Cap confirmed a false positive minutes later, and the markets were re-enabled after review.
Layer 2 · human-reviewed
The risk team, paged when it matters

Signals where acting automatically would cause false-positive disruption go to KPK's risk curation team, with a page for critical ones. A reviewer decides whether to force a rebalance, shut a market or do nothing. Monitoring is planned with each risk review, so the risks found on paper are the risks watched in production.

The agents
Health-factor managerkeeps HF between 1.30 and 1.90

Holds the BTC leg inside the band with a 1.80 target. Monitoring reads the health factor every block and wakes it the moment HF leaves the band; it also runs every 5 minutes as a backstop. KPK sets the band itself: tighter when markets are volatile, looser when they are calm. Below the repay trigger it withdraws USDC from the liquid sleeve and repays; above the borrow trigger it borrows and deploys, split across the two vaults.

Exit agent (emergency unwind)unwinds the whole loan

Withdraws from the vaults and repays all the debt in one transaction. It fires on its own if HF falls below 1.13, or if borrowing costs more than the vaults earn for two 6-hour windows in a row, and switches re-lever off so nothing borrows again until the team re-opens the position.

Agents on the KPK USDC vaultsrebalance, exit, monitoring

Each vault runs three agents through its own permissions layer. The rebalance agent reallocates across approved Morpho markets within tier and cap limits as APYs, flows and utilisation change. The exit agent de-risks a single market on a risk alert such as oracle staleness, liquidity stress, a collateral pause or price divergence. The monitoring agent detects forceDeallocate griefing and restores allocations.

Onchain permissions
No key can move the assets

The Safe holding the position is owned by a contract with no key. Every action passes through a Zodiac Roles permissions layer.

Whitelisted actions only

A fixed set of calls on approved contracts, such as borrowing on the Aave V4 spoke or depositing into the KPK USDC vaults. Anything else is rejected onchain. Each agent and the Manager Safe (2 of 5) get a narrower role.

3-day timelock on changes

KPK's Security Council proposes any change to the permissions, and it waits 3 days onchain before it can take effect.

Risks and terms

Main risks, and how they are handled

BTC price
Holders keep full BTC exposure; nothing is hedged.
This is the product: carry on top of the BTC held, not a hedge.
Liquidation
A BTC drop faster than the position can be deleveraged.
Monitoring reads the health factor every block. The agent repays from 1.30, where BTC would still have to fall 23% to reach liquidation, and the exit agent unwinds the whole loan below 1.13. KPK tightens the band when markets turn volatile.
Negative carry
Borrowing costs rise above what the vaults earn.
The exit agent unwinds if that lasts two 6-hour windows in a row.
Vault liquidity
Thin liquidity in the Morpho vaults can slow deleveraging and redemptions.
At least 25% of the BTC-leg debt stays in the liquid sleeve, and the vault agents rebalance as liquidity changes.
Oracles and pegs
Manipulation, staleness or failure of a price feed; WBTC or a stablecoin off its peg.
Every price is checked against independent monitor feeds, and settlement pauses on a stale or irregular price.
Smart contracts
Aave V4, Morpho, the stablecoins, the BTC wrappers (WBTC; cbBTC and cirBTC once live) and the fund's own contracts.
